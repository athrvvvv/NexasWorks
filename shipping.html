Shipping & Delivery Policy

Since NexasWorks is a digital web development agency, we do not ship physical products. All our services and products are delivered digitally.

1. Delivery Method

Upon completion of web development milestones or final projects, the digital assets (code, website access, design files) will be delivered electronically via secure email, GitHub, or direct server deployment as agreed upon in the project scope.

2. Delivery Timeframe

The estimated delivery timeframe depends on the specific service package purchased:

  • Starter Landing Page: Delivered within 5-7 business days.
  • Custom Business Website: Delivered within 14-21 business days.
  • E-commerce Setup: Delivered within 30-45 business days.

Timelines commence only after we have received all necessary requirements and initial deposits from the client.

3. Shipping Charges

As all deliverables are digital, there are absolutely no shipping or handling charges associated with any of our services.
